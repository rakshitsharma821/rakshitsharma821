# Third-party material used by the SVGs

## Fonts (embedded in every SVG as base64 WOFF2 subsets)
| Font | Used for | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bricolage Grotesque (ExtraBold, SemiBold static instances) | display + text | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-BricolageGrotesque.txt`) | https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage via npm `@fontsource/bricolage-grotesque` |
| JetBrains Mono (Medium) | mono | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt`) | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono via npm `@fontsource/jetbrains-mono` |

The fonts were subset to the Latin glyphs the SVGs use (no outlines were edited). The fonts are not sold on their own.

## Brand icons
All platform and technology marks come from **Simple Icons v13.21.0** (npm `simple-icons@13.21.0`, https://simpleicons.org), whose icon data is CC0 (`SimpleIcons-LICENSE-CC0.md`).
Marks used: C, C++, JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, React, Vite, Three.js, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Socket.io, WebRTC, Git, GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram.
LinkedIn is no longer in current Simple Icons releases (removed upstream); the v13.21.0 mark is used. Brand names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear only to identify the platforms/technologies.
Generic glyphs (location pin, graduation cap, sprout, cricket/gaming/football line art, lanyard clasp) are original vector drawings.
